# 划词查词 · 试一试

把鼠标放在下面任意一个英文单词上、划选它，释义卡片就会弹出来。

- The committee decided to **abandon** the original proposal.
- She was **happier** than she had been for years.
- He **took** the book and left without a word.
- The village lies **in spite of** all odds, still standing.

试试这些词：abandon、happier、took、resilient、ambiguous、serendipity

## 多词划选

拖选一整句甚至一整行，只会显示完整的词，半截词会被丢弃、虚词会被跳过：

The committee decided to abandon the original proposal yesterday.

（试着从 `committee` 中间拖到 `proposal` 中间，卡片里应该只剩 decided / abandon / original 这几个。）
